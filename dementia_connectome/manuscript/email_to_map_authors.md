We are using your openly shared DBM atrophy maps (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.7392621 and
10.5281/zenodo.10383493; CC-BY-4.0) in a network-modelling study of cortical atrophy in
Alzheimer's disease and the frontotemporal dementia syndromes, citing Dadar et al.,
*Neurobiology of Aging* 2022;111:54–63 and Metz et al. (CCNA 2024). A reviewer has asked
us to confirm some details that are not in the record descriptions, and we would be very
grateful for your help.

1. **Statistic in the FTD-variant maps.** The bvFTD, svPPA and pnfaPPA maps (record
   10383493) range from about −1.5 to +2.0, whereas the pooled FTD map from the same NIFD
   cohort (record 7392621) ranges from −16 to +14. Are the variant maps t-statistics, or a
   different quantity (for example Cohen's d, standardised betas, or t-values scaled by a
   constant)? (Our analyses are scale-invariant, but we want to describe the maps correctly.)
2. **Samples and controls.** How many patients and controls contributed to each map (AD,
   MCI, FTD, bvFTD, svPPA, pnfaPPA)? Do the three variant maps share one NIFD control group,
   and is the pooled FTD map the union of the three variants (or of a different FTD sample)?
3. **Model.** Which covariates were included (e.g. age, sex, site/scanner), were repeated
   scans per participant modelled (e.g. mixed effects with subject as a random effect), and
   what smoothing was applied to the Jacobian (or log-Jacobian) maps before the group model?
4. **ADNI selection.** Were the AD and MCI groups selected on amyloid status, or on
   clinical diagnosis only?
