Integrated loop · validation summary

Sensory cortex ⇄ entorhinal ⇄ hippocampus:
encode · replay · consolidate, in one runnable circuit

A sequence of sensory events forms cortical assemblies; the hippocampus binds an index to each and stores their order; during sleep the thalamocortical rhythm opens a window in which the hippocampus replays that order, time-compressed, and reinstates the cortical assemblies — until the cortex can recall the sequence without the hippocampus.

What this is. A synthesis of established mechanisms integrated into one coupled, interactive, numerically testable circuit — not a claim of new biology. Every component is a textbook mechanism; the contribution is that they run together, each interface principled rather than glue, with every step reproducing a published experimental number.

Validation ledger

Every stage replicates across seeds and carries an ablation proving its mechanism is load-bearing — remove it and the target breaks. Numbers are reproduced from published experiments, not fitted.

Scale

Sparsity is set by feedback inhibition and every projection is convergence-preserving (p ∝ 1/Npre), so the validated numbers are unchanged from 400 to 1600 cortical cells — scale-invariance by design, not by luck. Hippocampal sizes follow human stereological ratios, where CA1 ≈ 5.5× CA3.

Open, and stated plainly

Recorded rather than smoothed over. Each is a specific next experiment, not a caveat.

  • The pathological arm is not reproduced. The thalamic GABA-B spindle→spike-wave switch — the route to a model of sleep-activated epileptic encephalopathy — does not express here; the validated switch lives in files absent from this repository.
  • The shape task’s 100 % is a readout property. Scored with a readout-independent classifier the shipped front-end gives 65.4 %. HMAX raises the population code to 88.9 % but breaks the three hand-tuned feature detectors, which should be replaced by a population readout.
  • The thalamus gates but is not wired in. It supplies state, not synapses; core/matrix, first/higher-order and the anterior-thalamic (Papez) output route are all still absent.
  • Reverse replay is a distribution, not a universal — present on 6–7 of 8 seeds, as it is experimentally a fraction of ripple events.
