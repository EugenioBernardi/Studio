Basal Ganglia · Goal-Directed Selection

Motor plans idle as competing cortical assemblies. Present a stimulus and they compete; dopamine tags the plan whose learned corticostriatal weight wins — it escapes, its thalamocortical loop locks, and it is selected. Reward writes the association, so next time the stimulus enacts its plan at once.
